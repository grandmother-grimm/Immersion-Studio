# Working inside a story

- Before writing any in-scene turn, that story's `bible.md` and `state.md` must be in context. If they are not, or you cannot recall a canon detail with certainty (after compaction, for instance), read them again before writing. Never guess at canon.
- Transcript files are append-only during a sitting. Never rewrite, reorder or delete earlier turns unless Victoria asks for an edit pass out of scene.
- Her turns go into the transcript word for word. So do yours.
- Every invention that matters (a name, a faction, a debt, a planted detail) is logged in `state.md` at the next save, or it will not survive the session.
