## Where we are

Aboard the *Úlfsvaka*, second full day out from Merewic, just past midday, thin white sun through a high haze. The fleet (twenty-one prows and four knarrs, about eleven hundred men) was in sailing order heading north for Jarl Ott's fjord; it has just been ordered west instead.

The sea has gone dead flat and mirror-still. Every man's breath fogs. Off the starboard quarter, a long bowshot out, the ***Hrímfaxi*** — Steinn Ottsson's longship, lost twelve winters ago with the *Skinfaxi* and the *Naglfar* and a hundred and thirty men — has come out of the northern haze: strakes bleached white as bone (ice-burn), a torn grey sail bellied full *against* the wind and not fluttering, serpent prow carved by Kolbeinn, dark still shapes crowding her rails. Her oars, which had each rowed to a different beat, stopped together and came down together, and her bow is coming round toward the *Úlfsvaka*. At her prow stands a figure in grey sealskin, hood up, rooted against the roll. Beside it a **dead wolf** the size of Galdur: frost-furred over old blood, Ott's first spear snapped off in its heart, filmed blue eyes lit from behind. It has lifted its head and is **singing**, a note that does not end; a chorus answers from the haze to the north. The figure's hood has just turned, slowly, past the fleet and the Wolf, to Hrafna, and stopped on her.

**Frost handprints** are climbing over the *Úlfsvaka*'s gunwale from the dead water, bow to stern, one after another; the last has closed on the steering oar beside Hallr's hands. Ásgeir has backed away from the stem-post. Galdur stands across both Loki and Hrafna at the rail, every tooth bared, making a sound lower than a growl, eyes locked on his dead kin. Skuggi screamed one challenge across the water and now presses his head to Loki's neck, silent.

Loki has given the order: horn, one long and four short (Brandr blew it); every hull to break station and follow the flagship close; any captain who loses the stern keeps his bow on the sunset and his mouth shut; **no horns, no hails, no voice from any deck until Loki says**. Across the water Thorolf raised his arm in answer, Bersi came up off his knees, oars are coming down along the line. Kaleb has his bleeding hand over Mildrith's mouth (she bit him through; he said something to her in Norse, too low for Loki to hear, and her eyes changed). Hallr holds the oar with the frost beside his fingers.

Loki holds Hrafna against his chest, her spine burning black-blue through the pelt, and has just said: *"Call it, litla hrafn. Call your mist. Hide my ships. Take us west. I have the body. I have you. Whatever you have to spend, spend it. And then come back to me."* She has not yet called it.

West means the Hjaltland race in autumn gales, then the Orkneyjar, then grey sea; not victualled for Ísland; knarrs slow and loot-heavy; but nothing follows a fleet into the race, and they would come down on the fjord from the sea-side where no one watches, roughly a week out.
