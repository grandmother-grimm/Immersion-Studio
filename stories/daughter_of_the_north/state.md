## Where we are

**Skógey**, dawn of Day 3, at the head of the warm, steaming sound between the twin islands. The whole fleet is in the sound, still tied hull to hull on Kaleb's rope. Loki carried Hrafna off the *Úlfsvaka* first, waded ashore, and laid her on the moss at the root of the largest yew, a red giant with a hollow at its base like a lit hall-door; he pressed her hands into the earth and her fingers curled into it. The forest "breathed" (a sigh through the needles with no wind). There are fresh child-sized bare footprints with too-long toes running from the shingle into the trees; none come out. Loki knelt astride her with both seaxes drawn. Galdur walked to where the prints go in; from just behind the nearest trunk came a small, delighted child's laugh, and Galdur's tail moved once. No birds anywhere. The men have just come off the ships onto the shingle and stopped.

Hrafna is spent past her reserves: coughing blood (Jensen: not lungs, not a wound, "the cost"; she must stop spending and let the earth refill her), fever-heat, war-runes cold and hollow, wool scorched brown over them, the **shadow-marks lying still and sated**. Loki told her not to spend another word until her hands were in the earth.

Mildrith is gagged with her own hem and bound facing outward on the *Úlfsvaka*'s stem-post. Kaleb sits at her feet by choice and gave her water in the night. Loki has Hauk's knife in his belt "until the god-born decides what it is owed."
